Application of Learning
Task 1 — Translate the Phrase
🔓 Laptop gate. Each question uses a different letter for the unknown amount — read carefully before you write the expression.
🟢 Foundation
1. There are m marbles in a jar. 5 more marbles are added.
2. Sam has p pencils. He gives away 3.
3. A number, t, has 6 added to it.
4. There are k stickers on a page. 4 are removed.
5. A rope is w metres long. It is cut shorter by 2 metres.
6. There are c coins in a jar. 10 more are added.
7. A number, y, has 7 taken away from it.
8. A recipe uses f cups of flour. 2 more cups are added.
🟡 Developing
1. The product of x and 6.
2. n divided by 4.
3. 8 less than a number, d.
4. 5 subtracted from a number, q.
5. Half of a number, r.
6. The sum of a number, b, and 9.
7. z multiplied by 8.
8. A number, h, shared equally among 5 people.
🟣 Extending — two-step and reverse
1. A number, n, is doubled, then 3 is added.
2. Twice a number, p, decreased by 4.
3. A number, m, has 2 added, then the result is tripled.
4. Given the expression 4k + 7, write a worded scenario that matches it.
5. Given the expression (t − 5) ÷ 2, write a worded scenario that matches it.

Application of Learning
Task 3 — Build Your Own
One capstone task per tier — pick your tier (or try more than one).
🟢 Foundation
Model this scenario: “5 more than a number, k.” Draw the flow diagram, then write the expression.
→→
🟡 Developing
Write your own one-step worded scenario using multiplication or division, then diagram it.
→→
🟣 Extending — two-step chain
Chain two operations in one flow diagram (e.g. add, then multiply) for a number, n. Show both steps and the simplified expression.
