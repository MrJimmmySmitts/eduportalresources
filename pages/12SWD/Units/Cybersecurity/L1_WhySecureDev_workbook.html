Lesson 1 — Why Secure Software
Development Matters

Organisational goals and objectives, and the security trade-offs of developing software in-house versus externally.

1
Learning Objective
Where you're headed today

To explain how organisational goals and objectives shape software development decisions, and evaluate the security trade-offs of developing software in-house versus externally.

🟢 Foundation

I can define mission statement, organisational goal, and objective, and give one example of each. I can list one advantage and one disadvantage of developing software externally.

🟡 Developing

I can explain how a specific organisational goal might influence a decision to develop software in-house or externally, and connect a security-related advantage or disadvantage to a real consequence.

🟣 Extending

I can evaluate, for a given organisation, whether in-house or external development better serves its goals and security needs — with justification drawing on multiple factors.

🟢 Foundation

1. "Become the region's most trusted online retailer within 5 years."
2. "Reduce checkout cart abandonment by 15% by the end of this financial year."
3. "Migrate all customer records to the new database by 30 June."
4. "Improve overall student wellbeing across the school."
🟡 Developing

Organisation profile — GreenLeaf Logistics: a mid-sized freight company whose mission is "to move goods reliably while minimising our environmental footprint." They've had recent complaints about drivers receiving incorrect delivery addresses from their current booking system.

🟣 Extending

Compare two contrasting organisations: a small fintech start-up building a new payments app, and a public hospital replacing its patient-records system. For each, recommend in-house or external development, and justify your recommendation with reference to at least two specific advantages or disadvantages from Section 2 — not just a general statement.
