Application of Learning
Paper Tower Challenge
Activity

Build the tallest freestanding tower your group can, using only paper and tape. Work through Identify → Plan → Create → Evaluate as your teacher prompts each stage.

During the challenge
Identify: what did your group decide "success" meant before you started building?
Plan → Create: where did your group get stuck — was it a Plan problem or a Create problem?
Evaluate: if you could "Plan" again, what's the one thing you'd change?
