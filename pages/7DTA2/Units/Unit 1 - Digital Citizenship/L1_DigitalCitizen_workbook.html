Lesson 1: Digital Citizen — Workbook

What does it mean to be safe, responsible and effective online — and why does it matter beyond just you?

🎯
Stage 1
Learning Objective

To be able to... understand what it means to be a safe, responsible digital citizen, and describe appropriate online behaviour.

See the unit site for full notes on this topic →
1
Task 1
Rights and responsibilities brainstorm

With a partner, brainstorm as many digital rights and responsibilities as you can before writing your best ones below.

What should you be able to expect when you're online?
What do you owe to other people when you're online?
2
Task 2
Interland activity
On devices

Play through at least one Interland world at beinternetawesome.withgoogle.com, then answer below.

Choose the one you spent the most time on.
Describe the skill or habit, not just the game mechanics.
Think about a moment where you got something wrong or had to think carefully.
3
Task 3
Scenario check

Your teacher will show a scenario on the board. Decide what the appropriate response would be.

Refer back to the rights and responsibilities table from the unit site.
↺
Stage 4
Reflection
Think back
Think about one online decision you made this week — big or small.
Be honest — this isn't marked on whether the decision was "good," just on your thinking.
