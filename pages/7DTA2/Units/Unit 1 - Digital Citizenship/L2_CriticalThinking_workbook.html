Lesson 2: Critical Thinking — Workbook

Not everything on the internet is true — including some very convincing, very fake websites.

🎯
Stage 1
Learning Objective

To be able to... critically evaluate the credibility of online sources, and identify signs of a trustworthy or untrustworthy site.

See the unit site for full notes on this topic →
1
Task 1
Analyse DHMO.org
On devices

Visit dhmo.org as a class or in pairs. Work through the checklist from the unit site.

Tick every box that applies based on what you find on the site.
Use specific evidence from the site — don't just say "no."
2
Task 2
Analyse the Tree Octopus site
On devices

Visit the Pacific Northwest Tree Octopus site and repeat the process.

Tick every box that applies.
Think about design, photos, or writing style.
3
Task 3
Choose your own site

Find a third website on any topic that interests you and run the same checklist.

Paste the website name or URL.
Refer to at least two of the six checks from the unit site.
4
Task 4
Quick-fire round
On devices

Six short examples. Working on your own, decide Likely real or Likely fake for each — and name the ONE detail that gave it away. Move fast.

1. Council announcement: “Melbourne City Council approves plans for a new pocket park in Docklands, opening in 2027.”

2. Instagram caption: “🚨 URGENT: This ONE juice cured my classmate's asthma overnight — doctors don't want you to know!!”

3. Wikipedia-style snippet: “The Southern Alpine Fire Newt, a glowing amphibian discovered in Tasmania in 2003, is now considered extinct.”

4. Weather report: “The Bureau of Meteorology forecasts above-average rainfall for Victoria this spring due to La Niña conditions.”

5. Blog post title: “I ate only pineapple for 30 days and my eyesight improved by 200%.”

6. School newsletter: “Year 7 students placed 2nd in the regional Tournament of Minds competition last weekend.”

Stage 4
Peer-check
Swap workbooks
Give your workbook to a partner. Check at least one of their verdicts above: does it point to something specific, or just say "it seems fake"? Tell them if it's too vague.
If they flagged something vague, note what you'd add now.

Stage 5
Reflection

Connect this back to the checklist.
