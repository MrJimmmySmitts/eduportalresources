Lesson 3: Managing Digital Schoolwork — Workbook

A messy digital desktop costs you time all year. Today you build a system that will still make sense in Term 4.

Stage 1
Learning Objective

To be able to... organise and manage digital files and tools effectively, including logging in, organising folders, and creating shortcuts.

See the unit site for full notes on this topic →

Task 1
Login checklist
On devices

Work through each login and tick it off as you go.

Tick each account once you've successfully logged in.
2
Task 2
Build your folder structure
On devices

Create a subject folder for Digital Technology, and a unit folder inside it for Unit 1.

Check it matches the pattern from the unit site.
3
Task 3
Screenshot your folder structure
On devices

Take a screenshot showing your Digital Technology folder with the Unit 1 folder inside it, then upload it below as evidence.

See Slide 9 for the screenshot shortcut on your device. JPG or PNG.
4
Task 4
Rename a file

Rename one existing file (this workbook's export, or another school file) using the naming convention.

Use the Subject_Topic_ShortDescription pattern.
5
Task 5
Verify your backup
On devices

Check that your work is actually protected — not just organised.

Look carefully — is it a tick, a spinning arrow, a red mark, or something else?
6
Task 6
Bookmarks
On devices

Set up a bookmarks folder in your browser for school sites.

This is the extending challenge — explain the logic, not just the steps.

Task 7
What if today was the day?
Extending

Your teacher will pose this scenario to the class — write your own answer below.

Walk through it step by step — where would you actually look, and why would it work?
