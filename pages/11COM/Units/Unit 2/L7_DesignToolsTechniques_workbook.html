Developing · D5.1 Build Your Storyboard

Show the sequence, not just the screen

A storyboard shows how a user moves through your solution over time — what they see and do at each step. Aim for at least four panels covering a full interaction from start to finish.

Q4🟡 Developing

Build your storyboard panel by panel. For each panel, describe the scene, what the user does, and any notes on their context or reaction.

→ See Reference Site: Elements of an Effective Storyboard.
