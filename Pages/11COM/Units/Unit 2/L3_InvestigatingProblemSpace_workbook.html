Investigating the Problem Space

Digital Workbook — open at the Application of Learning phase only

📚 How to use this workbook

Use your paper notes and the lesson reference website to support your answers. Tasks are tiered: work through Foundation first, then Developing, then Extending if time allows. Your group discusses — you each write your own workbook.

→ Open the Lesson 3 Reference Website
1
Foundation · ~10 min
Existing Solutions Map
🟢 Foundation
📖 See Reference Website → Section 3

Use the "Analysing Existing Solutions" framework from the website and your paper notes. This task uses Questions 01 and 02 from the 4-question framework.

Task 1.1

Your Group's Topic Area

In one sentence, describe the topic area or domain your group is investigating.

Task 1.2

3 Existing Digital Solutions

Research and record at least 3 digital solutions that already address your topic area. For each, note the name, who it's for, what it does, and one limitation you can identify.

Solution Name Who is it for? What does it do? One limitation
2
Developing · ~10 min
User Profile
🟡 Developing
📖 See Reference Website → Section 4

Use the User Profile template from the reference website and your paper notes to guide this task.

Task 2.1

Identify Your Main User Group

Based on your topic area and existing solutions research, who has the problem you want to address? Complete the user profile below. Be specific — avoid describing "everyone" or "all people".

Task 2.2

How specific is your user group?

Rate the specificity of your user group so far. Be honest — it's fine if it's still broad at this stage.

3
Developing · ~10 min
Gap Analysis
🟡 Developing
📖 See Reference Website → Section 3, Questions 03 and 04

Use your existing solutions map (Task 1.2) and your user profile (Section 2) to complete this analysis.

Task 3.1

Identify the Key Gap

Looking at the limitations you identified in Task 1.2, and comparing them to what your user group actually needs — what is the most significant gap that existing solutions fail to address?

Task 3.2

Evidence for the Gap

What evidence supports the claim that this gap causes a real problem? This could be from your research, a review, a statistic, a news article, or your own observations.

4
Extending · ~10 min
First Draft Problem Statement
🔵 Extending
📖 See Reference Website → Section 5

Use the problem statement formula from the reference website. Your response here should build directly on your gap analysis and user profile from Sections 2 and 3.

[USER GROUP] need a way to [ACCOMPLISH GOAL]
because [INSIGHT ABOUT THE PROBLEM]
Task 4.1

Fill in the Formula

Use your research from Sections 1–3 to complete each part of the formula below. This is a first draft — it doesn't have to be perfect, but it should be grounded in your research.

Task 4.2

Your Complete Problem Statement

Write your group's full first-draft problem statement in a single, coherent sentence. Use your answers from Task 4.1 — don't just paste the formula with blanks filled in. Write it as a proper sentence.

5
Exit Ticket · ~7 min
Check Your Understanding
🎫 Exit Ticket
⚠️ No looking back at the website

These questions check what you remember from today's lesson. Q1 and Q2 are required. Q3 is Extending.

Exit Ticket Q1 · Foundation

Research Types

In your own words: what is the difference between primary research and secondary research? Give one example of each.

Exit Ticket Q2 · Developing

Your Problem Statement — From Memory

Without looking back at Section 4, write your group's first draft problem statement from memory. This tests whether the formula has stuck.

Exit Ticket Q3 · Extending

Justifying the Gap

Explain why your identified user group is specific enough, and identify what evidence you used to justify that the gap you found is real and significant.
