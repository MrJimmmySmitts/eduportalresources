4
Extending · D3.1 Data Collection Design Evaluating the Instrument
Q10🔵 Extending

What assumption is baked into your instrument that might not be true for all members of your user group? For example: assuming they have a smartphone; assuming they're comfortable reading long questions; assuming they already know what your project is about.

Think about who might be in your user group but different from your mental image of a "typical" user.

Q11🔵 Extending

If you could only ask your user group one question, which of your questions would you keep? Explain your reasoning — what does this question tell you that the others can't replace?
