🟢 Foundation

Getting 10% under control

Divide by 10. Watch the decimal point.

▸Now find the sale price of each item after 10% off.
Careful — the discount and the sale price are two different numbers. Which one is the question asking for?
🟡 Developing

Building any percentage

Use the 10% method. Show your building steps, not just the answer.

5Pick three deals from Section 2 and work out the percentage saved for each, with full working.
The saving needs to be compared to what you would normally have paid — not to what you actually paid.
🟣 Extending

Design a dodgy deal

Your job: write a shop sign that sounds amazing but saves people very little.

1Write the sign exactly as it would appear in the shop window.
2Now show the truth: normal price, actual price, and the real percentage saved.
3What is the trick your sign uses to make the saving sound bigger than it is?
🟣 Challenge

Deal B (buy 2 get 1 free) and Deal G (3 for the price of 2) came out identical at 33.3%. Invent a third way of wording that exact same deal, using completely different words again.
