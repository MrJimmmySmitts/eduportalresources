SECTION 5

Apply the criteria — the evidence pack

DEVELOPINGJUDGEMENT
Aurora's security manager has supplied the evidence below. For each item, reach a judgement and write a recommendation that responds to what the evidence actually shows.

5.1  Repository access

Repository member list: 14 accounts. HR staff list: 11 current employees. Three accounts belong to people whose contracts ended in the previous 12 months. No record exists of any access review having been carried out.

5.2  Encryption

Database configuration confirms encryption at rest is enabled on the customer database. The app connects to the server over TLS. The nightly marketing export writes a plain CSV of customer names and emails to a shared drive.

5.3  Patching

A patch management policy exists, dated three years ago, requiring critical patches within 14 days. Update logs show the last operating system patch on the build server was applied 26 months ago. Application dependencies were last updated four months ago.

5.4  Environment separation

A network diagram shows separate development, testing and production environments. Deployment records show 168 releases in 90 days. Interviews confirm developers use a copy of the live customer database in the testing environment "so the test data is realistic".

5.5  Rank your four recommendations in the order Aurora should act on them, and justify the order.
