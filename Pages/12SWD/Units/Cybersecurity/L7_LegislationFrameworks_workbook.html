SECTION 4

Which Act, which principle?

DEVELOPINGEXTENDED WRITTEN
Six organisations. For each: name the Act that applies, the specific principle at issue, and one sentence on the consequence. Check what kind of organisation it is before you decide which Act applies — that decision governs everything after it.

4.1  Meridian Freight — a logistics company, turnover $14 million. Driver licence numbers are used as the internal staff ID in every system.

4.2  Barrandale Shire Council — its resident reporting app stores photographs and addresses on a server located in Ireland. The contract with the hosting provider is silent on privacy.

4.3  Tallow Lane Dental — a two-chair practice with turnover of $480,000. Patient contact details collected for appointments are used to send promotional offers.

4.4  Kelp & Co — a five-person start-up, turnover $210,000. It ships a paid app built on a library whose licence requires derived work to be released under the same terms. Nobody read the licence.

4.5  Northbridge Water — a Victorian statutory authority. Its website has no privacy policy and has not published one since 2019.

4.6  Verity Systems — a private software house, turnover $6 million, building a case-management system under contract for a Victorian government department. Test environments are loaded with live departmental records.

4.7  Which of the six was hardest to decide, and what information would have made it straightforward?
